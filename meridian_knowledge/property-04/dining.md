# Meridian City Suites - Dining Outlets
- **Metropolitan Bistro**: Upscale European bistro serving dry-aged steaks, artisan pasta, and fine continental wines.
- **Skyline Rooftop Lounge**: Trendy 30th-floor rooftop bar offering craft mixology cocktails, sushi rolls, and panoramic city skyline views.
- **City Grind Coffee Bar**: Quick-service espresso bar featuring single-origin coffees, fresh sandwiches, and pastries.

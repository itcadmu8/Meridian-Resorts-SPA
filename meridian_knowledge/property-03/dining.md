# Meridian Hillside - Dining Outlets
- **Timberline Lodge & Grill**: Rustic fireside steakhouse serving dry-aged steaks, artisan cheese fondue, and local craft beers.
- **Summit View Lounge**: Panoramic mountain cocktail bar offering fireside drinks, mulled wine, and gourmet charcuterie boards.
- **Alpine Bakery & Cafe**: Cozy morning cafe featuring freshly baked pastries, hot chocolate, and espresso drinks.

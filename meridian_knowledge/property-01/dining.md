# Meridian Grand Resort - Dining Outlets
- **Oceanfront Seafood Grill**: Fine dining restaurant serving fresh daily catch, lobster, and curated international wine pairings.
- **Azure Sunset Lounge**: Open-air cocktail bar featuring signature tropical drinks, tapas, and live evening acoustic music.
- **The Grand Pavilion**: All-day international buffet offering interactive cooking stations, Asian delicacies, and Mediterranean wood-fired pizzas.

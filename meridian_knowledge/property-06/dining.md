# Meridian Coastal Retreat - Dining Outlets
- **Cliffside Seafood Terrace**: Dramatic cliff-top dining featuring oysters, grilled lobster, daily ocean catch, and sunset champagne service.
- **Tide & Bar**: Intimate lounge bar serving organic wines, coastal cocktails, and small-plate seafood tapas.
- **Pavilion Lounge**: Open-air seaside lounge offering afternoon tea, fresh pastries, and light lunch fare.

# Meridian Garden Spa - Dining Outlets
- **Flora & Fauna Kitchen**: Farm-to-table organic restaurant serving nutrient-rich plant-based bowls, wild seafood, and cold-pressed juices.
- **Botanical Tea House**: Serene indoor-outdoor tea sanctuary offering rare herbal infusions, matcha lattes, and vegan high tea.
- **Garden Terrace Grill**: Al-fresco garden dining featuring grilled ocean trout, organic salads, and biodynamic wines.

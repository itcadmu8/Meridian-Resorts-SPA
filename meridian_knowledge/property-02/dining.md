# Meridian Seabreeze - Dining Outlets
- **Coral Reef Cafe**: Health-conscious breakfast & juice bar serving organic smoothie bowls, avocado toasts, and shade-grown coffee.
- **Breeze Deck Dining**: Waterfront Pan-Asian fusion restaurant featuring sushi, grilled skewers, and fresh seafood curries.
- **Poolside Tiki Bar**: Casual open-air bar serving fruit smoothies, handcrafted rum cocktails, and artisanal brick-oven pizzas.
